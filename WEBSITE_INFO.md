## 4. Services Offered & Detailed Descriptions

### 1. Instant Loan
- **Description:** An instant loan provides quick access to funds, often approved within minutes. It’s typically offered online, requiring minimal documentation and fast processing. Interest rates can be high, so borrowers should carefully review terms before committing to ensure affordability.

### 2. Personal Loan
- **Description:** A personal loan is an unsecured loan used for various purposes, such as debt consolidation or home improvements. It typically has fixed interest rates and repayment terms. Borrowers should assess their financial situation before applying to ensure manageable repayments.

### 3. Business Loan
- **Description:** A business loan provides funding to support a company's operations, growth, or expansion. It can be used for purchasing equipment, managing cash flow, or scaling operations. Terms, interest rates, and requirements vary, depending on the lender and the business's financial health.

### 4. Home Loan
- **Description:** A home loan, or mortgage, helps individuals purchase property by borrowing funds from a lender. It usually features long-term repayment plans and fixed or variable interest rates. Borrowers must meet specific eligibility criteria and provide collateral, typically the home itself.

### 5. Used & New Car Loan
- **Description:** Drive home your dream car with flexible financing up to 90% on-road valuation. Enjoy lowest EMI tenures up to 7 years with quick paperless verification.

### 6. Mortgage Loan
- **Description:** A mortgage loan is a secured loan where property is used as collateral. It enables individuals to buy real estate by borrowing from a lender, typically repaid over long terms with fixed or adjustable interest rates. Defaulting risks losing the property.

## 5. Working Process (4 Steps)

1. **Step 1: Application Submit**
   - The loan process begins with submitting a completed application, including required personal, financial, and employment information.
2. **Step 2: Review & Verification**
   - Lenders carefully review the application, verifying all provided details, including income, credit score, and financial history.
3. **Step 3: Loan Approval**
   - Upon successful verification, the lender assesses eligibility and grants loan approval based on the applicant's creditworthiness and financial standing.
4. **Step 4: Loan Disbursement**
   - Once approved, the loan amount is disbursed to the borrower, typically via bank transfer, completing the loan process.
